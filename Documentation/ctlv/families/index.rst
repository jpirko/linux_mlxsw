==========================
CTLV family specifications
==========================

One page per CTLV family, generated from the specification the kernel
metadata, the UAPI headers and the userspace bindings are generated from.
A page describes what its family declares itself and links to its parent
for what it inherits, so the pages of a chain read together the way the
generated headers include one another.
